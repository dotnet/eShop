## 🎯 Usage by Role

### For First-Time Presenters
1. **Day 1**: Read [README.md](README.md) and [PRESENTATION_PLAN.md](PRESENTATION_PLAN.md)
2. **Day 2**: Study [PRESENTATION_SCRIPT.md](PRESENTATION_SCRIPT.md) and [FEATURE_SPECIFICATION.md](FEATURE_SPECIFICATION.md)
3. **Day 3**: Follow [SETUP_GUIDE.md](SETUP_GUIDE.md) completely
4. **Day 4**: Practice with [PROMPT_TEMPLATES.md](PROMPT_TEMPLATES.md)
5. **Day 5**: Present with [QUICK_REFERENCE.md](QUICK_REFERENCE.md) open

### For Experienced Presenters
1. Skim [README.md](README.md) for overview
2. Jump to [SETUP_GUIDE.md](SETUP_GUIDE.md) for technical setup
3. Review [PROMPT_TEMPLATES.md](PROMPT_TEMPLATES.md)
4. Customize [PRESENTATION_SCRIPT.md](PRESENTATION_SCRIPT.md) to your style
5. Use [QUICK_REFERENCE.md](QUICK_REFERENCE.md) as reminder during presentation

### For Planning Committees
- Share [PRESENTATION_PLAN.md](PRESENTATION_PLAN.md) for approval
- Reference [FEATURE_SPECIFICATION.md](FEATURE_SPECIFICATION.md) for technical depth
- Time allocation is in [QUICK_REFERENCE.md](QUICK_REFERENCE.md)

## 🎓 Learning Path by Experience Level

### Beginner with TDD
**Focus**: Understand the methodology first
1. Read about TDD: https://martinfowler.com/bliki/TestDrivenDevelopment.html
2. Study [FEATURE_SPECIFICATION.md](FEATURE_SPECIFICATION.md) test scenarios
3. Follow [PRESENTATION_SCRIPT.md](PRESENTATION_SCRIPT.md) exactly
4. Practice 2-3 times before presenting
5. Use [QUICK_REFERENCE.md](QUICK_REFERENCE.md) heavily during presentation

### Intermediate Developer
**Focus**: Understand agent orchestration
1. Skim [PRESENTATION_PLAN.md](PRESENTATION_PLAN.md) for structure
2. Deep dive into [PRESENTATION_SCRIPT.md](PRESENTATION_SCRIPT.md) agent interactions
3. Customize [PROMPT_TEMPLATES.md](PROMPT_TEMPLATES.md) for your repo
4. Practice 1-2 times
5. Add your own insights during presentation

### Advanced/Architect
**Focus**: Demonstrate sophisticated workflows
1. Review [PRESENTATION_PLAN.md](PRESENTATION_PLAN.md) for goals
2. Adapt [FEATURE_SPECIFICATION.md](FEATURE_SPECIFICATION.md) to your domain
3. Customize [PRESENTATION_SCRIPT.md](PRESENTATION_SCRIPT.md) heavily
4. Create additional demo scenarios
5. Use as framework, improvise based on audience

## 🔀 Adaptation Scenarios

### Shorter Presentation (30 minutes)
**Use**: [PRESENTATION_PLAN.md](PRESENTATION_PLAN.md) + [QUICK_REFERENCE.md](QUICK_REFERENCE.md)
- Cut Phase 5 (Orchestration)
- Reduce Phase 4 to 10 minutes
- Focus on Red/Green demonstration

### Longer Workshop (2 hours)
**Use**: All documents + hands-on time
- First hour: Follow full presentation
- Second hour: Attendees try with guidance
- Have [PROMPT_TEMPLATES.md](PROMPT_TEMPLATES.md) ready to share

### Virtual vs. In-Person
**Both use same docs, different tactics:**
- Virtual: Share screen of [QUICK_REFERENCE.md](QUICK_REFERENCE.md) briefly
- In-Person: Project [QUICK_REFERENCE.md](QUICK_REFERENCE.md) on side screen
- Virtual: Pre-record backup version
- In-Person: Have printed [QUICK_REFERENCE.md](QUICK_REFERENCE.md)

## 🎨 Customization Guide

Want to adapt this for your specific needs?

### Change Feature
1. Rewrite [FEATURE_SPECIFICATION.md](FEATURE_SPECIFICATION.md) for new feature
2. Update test scenarios in [PRESENTATION_SCRIPT.md](PRESENTATION_SCRIPT.md)
3. Adjust [PROMPT_TEMPLATES.md](PROMPT_TEMPLATES.md) to match new requirements
4. Update talking points in [QUICK_REFERENCE.md](QUICK_REFERENCE.md)

### Change Duration
- **30 min**: Cut Phases 5, reduce Phase 4
- **45 min**: Cut Phase 5
- **90 min**: Add refactoring phase, more Q&A
- **2 hours**: Add hands-on workshop

Update timing in [QUICK_REFERENCE.md](QUICK_REFERENCE.md) accordingly.
